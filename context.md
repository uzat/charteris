# Charteris — Business Context & Strategic Architecture

## Core Value Proposition
Charteris operates at the intersection of **property management compliance** and **high-end hospitality monetization**.

1. **For Property Agencies:** Reduces operational burden by automating 80% of guest calls and mitigating municipal noise fine risks, while turning cost-center properties into passive profit drivers via experience revenue sharing.
2. **For Property Owners:** Covers software costs via cashback earnings while protecting listing permits.
3. **For Guests:** Provides turn-key, friction-free luxury experiences and instant house manual assistance.

## Targeted Demographics & Primary Region
- **Launch Region:** Mornington Peninsula, Victoria, Australia (Sorrento, Portsea, Blairgowrie, Red Hill, Main Ridge).
- **Target Property Type:** High-end short-stay estates ($600–$2,000+/night) sleeping 6–12 guests.
- **Guest Profile:** High-net-worth groups, family reunions, girls' weekends, milestone celebrations.

## Unit Economics & Financial Target
- **SaaS Subscription:** $69 AUD / month per property (Pro Tier).
- **Affiliate Commission:** 15% average gross cut on experience GMV (Heli transfers @ $2,500 AUD, Private Chefs @ $1,200 AUD, Art Workshops @ $650 AUD).
- **Commission Split:** 50% retained by Platform, 50% passed back to Host/Agency.
- **Target Threshold for Founder Full-Time Transition ($11k/mo Net Income):** ~80 to 90 active properties (~1 to 2 agency partnerships).
- **Target Exit Valuation:** $50 Million AUD at 3,500–5,000 active properties nationwide (8x ARR multiple).

## Physical Artifact Design Standards
- **Timber Base:** Local reclaimed Australian oak block.
- **Display Plate:** Laser-cut clear acrylic with UV-printed typography and QR code.
- **Branding & Aesthetics:** Coastal luxury palette (slate navy, soft sand, white), featured botanical artwork by local artist Mel Bloom (Banksia/Eucalyptus motifs).
- **Local Fabrication Partners:** Southern Buoy (Mornington) & Signforce (Tyabb).
